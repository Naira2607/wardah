About Wardah

Wardah started with a small bunch of jasmine and a folded note. Our founder gave them to her grandmother, and her grandmother kept the note for years, even after the flowers dried.

That is when she noticed that people remember what was written with the flowers, not only the flowers. So we decided to include a handwritten card with every order.

Our History

  • 2019: We opened a small stall with twelve flower arrangements.
  • 2021: We started adding a handwritten card to every order.
  • 2023: We moved to our own studio.
  • 2026: We now make hundreds of orders every month.

What we care about

  • Fresh flowers from local growers, never older than two days.
  • Cards written by hand, not printed.
  • Paper and ribbon wrapping, no plastic.
