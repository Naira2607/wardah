What do flowers mean?

People have used flowers to send messages for a very long time. This table can help you choose the right one.

Flower Meaning Good for
Rose Love and admiration Anniversaries, thank you
Tulip Perfect love, cheerfulness Birthdays, spring
Peony Good luck, happy marriage Weddings, apologies
Sunflower Loyalty and joy Cheering someone up, graduation
Lavender Calm and peace Stressful times, thank you
Daisy Innocence, new beginnings New babies, small surprises
Lily Purity and renewal New homes, congratulations
Jasmine Grace and affection Mothers, brides

Not sure what to pick? Tell us how you feel and we will choose for you.
